## 🛡️ Team Performance Audit

### 🏆 Hanwha Life Esports (The Super Team)
*   **Roster:** Zeus, Kanavi, Zeka, Gumayusi, Delight.
*   **Key Stats:** 65% First Blood, 72% First Tower, 58% Dragon Control.
*   **Analysis:** The acquisition of Zeus and Gumayusi from T1 has created a stylistic juggernaut. They lead the league in almost every early-game metric.
*   **Betting Outlook:** Heavy favorites in almost every matchup. Look for "First Blood" and "Under 30 Minutes" bets.

### ⚡ KT Rolster (The Giant Slayers)
*   **Roster:** PerfecT, Cuzz, Bdd, Aiming, Ghost/Pollu.
*   **Analysis:** KT has been the surprise of the split, taking series off both Gen.G and T1. Bdd is currently playing at an MVP level.
*   **Betting Outlook:** Excellent value on Map 1 wins, as they have the highest Map 1 win rate in the league (90%).

### 👑 Gen.G Esports (The Return of the King)
*   **Roster:** Kiin, Canyon, Chovy, Ruler, Duro.
*   **Analysis:** Ruler's return has stabilized their late-game teamfighting, but they have shown vulnerability in the early game (only 45% FB rate).
*   **Betting Outlook:** "Over" on game duration. They are comfortable playing slow and winning through Chovy/Ruler's superior macro.

### 🔴 T1 (The Transition Phase)
*   **Roster:** Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz, Keria.
*   **Analysis:** Struggling with objective control (21% Dragon rate). The departure of Zeus and Gumayusi has clearly affected their synergy.
*   **Betting Outlook:** High variance. Avoid betting on them as heavy favorites until their objective control stabilizes.

## 📈 Meta & Betting Data
*   **Side Advantage:** Blue Side (55% Win Rate). Teams with first pick are currently benefiting heavily from the Fearless Draft format.
*   **Dragon Priority:** Lower than 2025. Teams are trading early Dragons for Grubs/Larvae at a higher rate (HLE leads in 6-Grub takes).
*   **Game Time:** Average 31:42. The meta is slightly slower than the LPL, favoring scaling compositions.
