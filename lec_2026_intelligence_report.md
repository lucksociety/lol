# 🇪🇺 LEC 2026 Spring Intelligence Report

The LEC 2026 Spring Split has been defined by a stark divergence between the "Elite Tier" (Karmine Corp, Team Vitality, NAVI) and the "Struggling Tier" (SK, Team Heretics). **Blue Side** selection remains a critical factor with a **56.96% win rate**, suggesting that side-selection priority in Best-of-3/5 series is a high-value betting variable.

## 🏆 Team-by-Team Intelligence

### 🔷 Tier S: The Contenders

#### **Karmine Corp (KC)**
*   **Current Record:** 6 - 0
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (9.8/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Closer". KC has the highest **MLR (+27.1)** in the league. Even if they fall behind early (GD15 +388 is good but not leading), they possess elite teamfighting and macro. 
*   **Key Stat:** 50% First Dragon control ensures they dictate the mid-game tempo.
*   **Betting Tip:** High confidence in "Match Winner" even at low odds. Look for "KC to Win Map 2" if they lose Map 1 (rare).

#### **Team Vitality (VIT)**
*   **Current Record:** 6 - 1
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (9.5/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Steamroller". Boasting a league-high **72% First Blood** and **GD15 +1037**, Vitality wins through early-game dominance.
*   **Key Stat:** EGR 61.7 (1st in LEC).
*   **Betting Tip:** Strongest candidate for "First Blood" and "Race to 5 Kills" bets.

### 🔶 Tier A: High-Volatility Threats

#### **Natus Vincere (NAVI)**
*   **Current Record:** 5 - 1
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (8.7/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Macro Kings". Highest **GD15 (+515)** and solid FT% (68%). They play a very structured game but have a neutral MLR (+0.1), meaning they don't "throw" but don't "clutch" as hard as KC.
*   **Betting Tip:** Very safe for "First Tower" bets.

#### **Movistar KOI (MDK)**
*   **Current Record:** 4 - 2
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (8.2/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Aggressive Opportunists". Solid EGR (56.4) and MLR (10.3). They are balanced and dangerous on both sides of the map.
*   **Betting Tip:** Good value for "Over 12.5 Towers Destroyed" in their matches due to their siege-heavy playstyle.

### 🟩 Tier B: The Gatekeepers

#### **GiantX (GX)**
*   **Current Record:** 3 - 3
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (7.1/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Early Specialists". They have a positive GD15 (+227) but a low FT% (27%). They win lanes but struggle to rotate efficiently for structures.
*   **Betting Tip:** Avoid "First Tower" bets on GX; favor "First Blood" if they are against lower-tier teams.

#### **G2 Esports**
*   **Current Record:** 3 - 4
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (6.8/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Late-Game Gamble". Despite a poor record and negative GD15 (-23), they have a high **MLR (+14.3)**. They are often overvalued by bookmakers due to their brand name.
*   **Betting Tip:** Good for "Live Betting" if they are down 2-3k gold at 15 mins—their comeback potential is higher than their odds usually suggest.

### 🟥 Tier C: The Trap Teams

#### **Fnatic (FNC)**
*   **Current Record:** 2 - 5
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐ (4.5/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Early Aggro Trap". **67% First Blood** but a negative MLR (-7.6). They win the early game and lose the game.
*   **Betting Tip:** Bet "First Blood" Fnatic, but bet *against* them for the "Match Winner" if they are against Tier S/A teams.

#### **Shifters (SHI)**
*   **Current Record:** 2 - 4
*   **Rating:** ⭐⭐ (4.2/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** The "Fading Stars". One of the worst MLRs in the league (-24.9). They completely fall apart after the 20-minute mark.
*   **Betting Tip:** Target for "Under 32.5 Minutes" when they lose, or "Opponent to win Map" after SHI gets an early lead.

---

### 💀 Tier D: The Bottom Feeders

#### **SK Gaming**
*   **Current Record:** 1 - 6
*   **Rating:** ⭐ (2.5/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** "Statistically Overwhelmed". League-worst **GD15 (-1496)**. They lose every lane nearly every game.
*   **Betting Tip:** Automatic "Opponent -1.5 Handicap" (if Bo3) or simply bet against them in every "Race to 5/10 Kills".

#### **Team Heretics (TH)**
*   **Current Record:** 0 - 6
*   **Rating:** ⭐ (1.8/10)
*   **Betting Profile:** "The Collapse". Despite a decent FB% (63%), they have an atrocious MLR (-24.8). They are the ultimate "Lead-Throwers".
*   **Betting Tip:** The "Fade" team of the split. Bet against them regardless of early-game momentum.

## 🔮 Betting Recommendations Summary
1.  **The Blue Advantage:** In matches between Tier A teams (MDK vs NAVI), prioritize the team on the Blue side.
2.  **Vitality's Bloodlust:** VIT for First Blood is the most consistent bet in the LEC currently.
3.  **KC's Invincibility:** Bet on KC ML (Moneyline) even at low odds for parlay builders.
4.  **The Fnatic Trap:** Fnatic for First Blood / Opponent for Match Winner is a high-value "hedge" strategy.
5.  **Targeting SK:** SK is currently the safest team to bet against for "First Tower" and "First 10 Kills".
